Account Deletion Request

We're sorry to see you go. You can request deletion of your SOS Emergency App account and all associated data at any time. This page explains what will be deleted and how to submit your request.

⚠️ Important: This Action Cannot Be Undone

Once your account is deleted, all your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. This includes:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • All emergency contacts you've added
  • SOS alert history
  • App settings and preferences
  • Any uploaded profile photos

You will not be able to recover this data after deletion.

What Gets Deleted?

When you delete your account, we will permanently remove:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Emergency Contacts: All contacts you've added to your emergency list
  • Location Data: Any stored location information from past alerts
  • Alert History: All previous SOS alerts and their details
  • Account Credentials: Your login information and authentication tokens
  • App Settings: All preferences and customizations

ℹ️ What About My Emergency Contacts?

Your emergency contacts will not be affected. Deleting your account only removes the contact information stored in your app. Your contacts' own accounts and data remain intact.

Settings → Profile → Delete Account

2Email Us Your Request (Current Method)

Send an email to request account deletion. Please include your registered email address so we can identify your account.

📧 Send Deletion Request Email

Email: info@chameleon-ideas.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - SOS Emergency App

Include in email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Confirmation that you understand data cannot be recovered

Response Time: We'll process your request within 48 hours and confirm via email when deletion is complete (typically within 30 days).

Deletion Timeline

Here's what happens after you submit your deletion request:

  1. Immediate (Within 24 hours): Your account is deactivated and you can no longer log in
  2. Within 7 days: Active data is removed from production systems
  3. Within 30 days: All backups and archived data are permanently deleted
  4. Confirmation: You'll receive an email when deletion is complete

Alternative: Delete Specific Data

If you don't want to delete your entire account, you can delete specific data instead:

  • Emergency Contacts: Remove individual contacts in the app
  • Profile Photo: Delete or change your profile photo in Settings
  • Alert History: Contact us to delete specific alerts

For partial data deletion requests, email us at info@chameleon-ideas.com with details of what you'd like removed.

Data Retention for Legal Purposes

In rare cases, we may need to retain certain data for legal compliance, such as:

  • Records required by law enforcement or legal proceedings
  • Transaction records required for tax or audit purposes
  • Data needed to resolve disputes or enforce our terms

Any data retained for these purposes will be kept securely and deleted as soon as the legal obligation ends.
