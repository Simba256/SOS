Privacy Policy

SOS Emergency App
Last Updated: December 13, 2025
Important Notice: SOS Emergency App is designed to assist you in emergency situations. This app should not replace official emergency services. Always call your local emergency number (911, 112, etc.) for immediate assistance.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SOS Emergency App ("we," "our," or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you create an account and use our App, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Emergency Contacts: Names and phone numbers of your emergency contacts
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials and authentication tokens

2.2 Location Data

Our App collects precise location data to enable emergency features. This includes:

  • Real-time GPS location: Used when you trigger an SOS alert
  • Background location: May be collected when the app is in background mode during an active emergency
  • Location history: We do not store your location history beyond active emergency sessions

2.3 Usage Data

  • App usage statistics and analytics
  • Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers)
  • Crash reports and performance data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Emergency Alerts: To send your location and emergency alerts to your designated emergency contacts
  • App Functionality: To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and secure your account
  • Communication: To send you important notifications about your account or the App
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the App and improve user experience
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security vulnerabilities

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Emergency Contacts

When you trigger an SOS alert, your location and emergency information will be shared with your designated emergency contacts via SMS or other communication methods you've enabled.

4.2 Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate our App:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, storage, and analytics
  • SMS Services: For sending emergency text messages
  • Cloud Services: For data storage and processing

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or if we believe such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations.

4.4 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure Storage: Your data is stored on secure Firebase servers with access controls
  • Authentication: We use secure authentication methods to protect your account
  • Access Controls: Only you can access your emergency contacts and personal information

However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Emergency Alerts: Active alert data is retained for 30 days, then automatically deleted
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained for service improvement

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access your personal information through the App settings
  • Correction: You can update or correct your information at any time
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: You can request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: You can disable location services, though this will limit emergency features

7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

7.2 CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or shared
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

8. App Permissions

Our App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Location (Always): To share your location during emergencies, even when the app is in the background
  • Contacts: To allow you to select emergency contacts from your phone
  • SMS: To send emergency text messages to your contacts
  • Internet: To sync data and send emergency alerts
  • Wake Lock: To keep the app active during emergency situations
  • Camera: To allow you to set a profile photo (optional)

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. However, disabling critical permissions may limit the App's emergency functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.
